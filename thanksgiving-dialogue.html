Thanksgiving Dialogue Gathering

What Do We Mean by God?

A time to express gratitude to God for family, friends, and the blessings of care and protection.

Saturday, November 14, 2026 · 5:00 PM Central

St. Timothy’s Anglican Church
6819 Louetta Road, Spring, TX 77379

Program

  1. Welcome · Dr. Johnson Petta
  2. Prayer · Mr. Dustin James
  3. Introduction · Fr. Nazir
  4. Speakers’ reflections · 5–7 minutes each
  5. Two personal testimonies

Reflection questions

  • How has my faith shaped my understanding of God?
  • Where do I recognize God’s presence in everyday life?
  • How have times of joy or hardship shaped my relationship with God?
  • What blessings do I overlook, and how can I give thanks for them?
  • How can my gratitude become acts of kindness, forgiveness, and service?
  • What can I learn by listening to people whose faith differs from mine?

Testimony theme

Experiencing God’s presence in everyday life and in times of crisis.

Leaders’ recording

“What does Thanksgiving mean to you in one sentence?”
