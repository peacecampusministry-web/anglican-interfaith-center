Peace Campus Ministry 2021 Annual Report

Fr. Nazir Masih

Working for peace

When people know little about their neighbors’ beliefs and ways of life, mistrust can grow. Honest conversation gives people room to speak about their own traditions and to hear others with respect. Paul calls Christians to pursue what leads to peace and builds others up (Romans 14:19).

Outreach during the pandemic

Over the past year, God gave us opportunities to share the Gospel across Texas, both in person and over Zoom. Anglican Peace Campus Ministry partnered with Baptist Student Ministry at the University of Houston, which made space available for us to meet with international students. We began offering a free monthly lunch that drew 50 to 80 students, and we held a student Bible study every Tuesday.

Visiting our neighbors

We visited mosques and temples to build relationships and learn how our neighbors practice their faith. Those visits gave us opportunities to deepen mutual understanding and share the Gospel.

Thanks and prayer

I thank God for our interfaith team: Fr. Larry, Frank Boyd, Jim Boggs, Dele, Ify, Kurt, Lucinda, Johnson, and Dick. I also thank Bishop Clark and Fr. Stan for giving me the opportunity to serve. Please pray with me that the Holy Spirit will guide our work as we seek to serve God’s kingdom.
