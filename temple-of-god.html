The Temple of God

Paul’s image of the Church as God’s temple invites believers to consider how the Spirit’s presence shapes their life together. This reflection is available in English and Urdu.

English

Paul reminds believers that they are God’s temple and that the Spirit of God dwells in them (1 Corinthians 3:16). The Church is God’s temple, and God also dwells in the hearts of believers.

God’s presence in us is where righteousness and peace come together. As we serve others, God gives us the wisdom and strength to remain in Him. In His presence, the Church finds unity and remains faithful as it awaits Christ’s return.
