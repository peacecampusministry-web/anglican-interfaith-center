Iftar Dinner at St. Timothy’s Anglican Cathedral

Fr. Nazir Masih, Diocesan Evangelist

On March 2, the Anglican community welcomed guests from a range of cultural and religious backgrounds to St. Timothy’s Anglican Cathedral for Iftar, the evening meal that breaks the daily Ramadan fast. Christians and Muslims were both observing seasons of fasting, giving guests a starting point for a conversation about faith, respect, and reconciliation.

Questions around the table

During the meal, Dr. Johnson introduced five questions for discussion:

  1. Who are you?
  2. How can we respect one another as human beings?
  3. What can help us learn from one another?
  4. What does reconciliation mean?
  5. How can we come together?

Fasting and spiritual life

Fr. Nazir spoke about fasting as a spiritual practice that draws us closer to God. He explained that fasting can renew our hearts and minds, help us listen for what God asks of us, and deepen the way we relate to others. He closed by inviting participants to consider how learning to love themselves can help them trust God and embrace their neighbors.

Thank you to everyone who shared the meal and took the time to get to know someone new.
