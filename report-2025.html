Peace Campus Ministry 2025 Report

Fr. Nazir Masih, Diocesan Evangelist

In 2025, Peace Campus Ministry gave students a place to pray, study Scripture, and form friendships amid the demands of university life. The year also saw the growth of an Urdu-language worship service and continued outreach to neighboring faith communities.

Faith and fellowship on campus

Peace Campus Ministry supports students in their academic and spiritual lives. With classes, work, and personal responsibilities competing for their attention, students have a place to pray, worship, study Scripture, and build friendships.

In 2025, we saw students come to faith in Christ and others return to their faith. Some reconnected with their families, pastors, and congregations. Students also took part in Bible studies, campus fellowships, and worship in their local churches.

Urdu worship services

In December 2024, we began a monthly Urdu-language worship service so people could praise God and share fellowship in a language close to their hearts. About 15 to 20 people attended each month. We later established a weekly Urdu service. Long travel distances and a small local Pakistani community remain challenges, and we trust God to guide the ministry as it grows.

Interfaith dialogue

We reached out to local mosques, gurdwaras, temples, and other religious communities to make room for conversation and cooperation. Through these relationships, we seek to deepen mutual respect, work for peace, and share the love of Jesus Christ with our neighbors.

Gratitude and prayer

I thank Bishop Clark Lowenfield, Fr. Stan Gerber, the Cathedral clergy, the diocesan clergy, our parishes, and everyone who has prayed for and supported this ministry. We trust God to give us the strength, wisdom, and resources to serve faithfully. Please continue to pray for the students and communities we serve.

In Christ,
Fr. Nazir Masih
